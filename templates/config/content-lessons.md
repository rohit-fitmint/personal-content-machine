# Content Lessons

The machine's memory of mistakes. `/content-machine:lessons` appends here after you post, by diffing
your published version against the machine-original first draft. `/content-machine:draft` and
`/content-machine:council` read these as hard rules. Keep each lesson short, specific, and generalizable.

Format per lesson:
`- [YYYY-MM-DD] <rule>. (evidence: what you changed)`

## Anti-slop starters (seeded — universal)
These ship on day one so drafts aren't slop before the loop has learned your quirks.
- Kill the intro. No "In today's fast-paced world", "As a founder", "Let me break this down".
- No adjective stacks. "A simple, powerful, elegant tool" → pick one or cut all.
- Ban corporate verbs: leverage, utilize, unlock, empower, streamline, supercharge, delve.
- No hollow CTAs: "Thoughts? 👇", "Agree?", "Retweet if…".
- Specifics > abstractions. "37 tests" beats "comprehensive test coverage".
- One idea per post. If there are two, it's two posts.
- Don't explain the joke or summarize what you just said ("In short, …").
- Show the mess. The struggle/wrong-turn is more interesting than the clean result.
