# Idea Dump

Your private backlog of raw thoughts you want to write about. The Oracle reads this **first** and
gives these ideas the top slots. One idea per bullet — a rough phrase is fine, it doesn't need to be
polished. This is the local fallback for the priority source; you can switch to a Slack channel in
`config/oracle-sources.md` (Option B) if you prefer dumping ideas there.

Window: the Oracle looks back **30 days** here (an exception to its 7-day rule — this is a backlog,
not events). Ideas you've clearly already posted are skipped.
